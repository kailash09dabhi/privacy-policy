Key Permissions

  • Set Wallpaper: Required to apply the live wallpaper to your home or lock screen.
  • Internet Access: Used to fetch occasional updates for the wallpaper content and serve advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • Vibration: Optional feedback for interactive elements within the app.

Data Security & Security Practices

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transferred between your device and Google services (like AdMob/Firebase) is encrypted using modern protocols (TLS/SSL).
  • Data Deletion: Since we do not collect personal identifiers (name, email, phone), there is no account data to delete. However, you can resets your Advertising ID anytime in your Android System Settings.
  • Third-Party Privacy: We use Google Play Services. For more details on how they handle data, please visit their Privacy Policy.
