Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications developed by Kailash Dabhi and published on the Google Play Store. I am committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience while using my apps.

My apps are designed to be functional and lightweight. I do not collect personal identification information such as your name, email address, or phone number unless you explicitly provide it to me for support purposes.

Data Collection & Usage

Depending on which app you use, certain data may be accessed to provide features:

  • Storage Access: Used to save wallpapers, export audio recordings, or access local music files for playback.
  • Microphone: Specifically for apps like OmRecorder, used only when you initiate a recording. Your audio is processed locally and never uploaded to any servers.
  • Device IDs: We use Google Advertising IDs (AAID) to serve personalized advertisements through AdMob and to analyze app performance.
